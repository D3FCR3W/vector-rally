# Replays and video export

[Back to the game overview](../README.md)

## Watch a replay

After at least one turn, select **▶** in the top bar. You can also choose **Watch full replay** after the race.

Replay includes every recorded turn, jump, crash, penalty and retirement. Cars animate together by race round. Your live race stays intact so you can return to your turn.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Play / pause or Space | Start or pause playback |
| Timeline | Seek through the recording |
| Previous / next or Left / Right | Step by round |
| Speed | Choose 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× or 4× |
| Show driven paths | Display the routes travelled up to the current time |
| Back to race or Escape | Restore the live race and camera |

At 1×, one round takes one second. Recordings last until a new race or page reload. Playback frame rate depends on your browser and display.

## Export in one click

Choose **Export video** from the replay panel or finish screen. The completed file downloads automatically.

The video includes the entire recording at **1280 × 720, 60 fps and 4× speed**, with a mini-map, driver names, condition and race statistics. It has a one-second opening and a two-second result card. No sound is recorded.

Before exporting, choose **Video camera**:

- **Follow a driver** keeps the selected participant in close view. By default, this is the first human in the starting grid, regardless of who won; an all-AI race defaults to the first car.
- **All drivers** adjusts the camera to keep every car visible as they spread out.

The replay and finish screens share this choice. Starting a new race restores the default. Switching to All drivers preserves your individual selection for switching back.

## Effects and timing

Export uses 4× speed independently of the replay controls. If **Show driven paths** is enabled, those paths appear in the video.

Videos retain full driving effects even if reduced motion is enabled for live play. Recorded wildlife motion is reproduced continuously. If a recording contains only frozen scenery snapshots, export animates the creatures visually without changing recorded collisions or results. Switching living scenery off hides them.

Frames are generated individually. A slower computer takes longer to prepare the video without changing its frame rate or playback duration.

## Save, share or cancel

Keep the tab open while exporting. Switching to another tab pauses generation until you return. **Cancel export** discards unfinished output and returns you to the game.

Once ready, **Download video** saves the file again. **Share video** appears when your browser supports sharing files through the system share sheet.

MP4 is preferred, with WebM as a fallback. Browsers without compatible video encoding show an explanation. An encoding failure leaves the race intact and offers a way back. Refreshing or leaving the page cancels unfinished work; files already downloaded remain saved.
